Cello from scratch

1/8 size

Let the app hear your cello

Everything here works by listening: the tuner reads the pitch of each string, and the lessons listen to you play and tell you what to change.

Nothing is recorded and nothing leaves your device. All the analysis happens on your phone.

Your browser will ask for permission. If you have refused it before, tap the padlock next to the web address, switch the microphone on for this site, then reload.

Built for a 1/8 size cello. Works offline once loaded.
